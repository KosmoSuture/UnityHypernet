# Quick Start — for the attorney

You stay the responsible lawyer. This just makes your review fast and targeted. Five steps:

1. **Pick the tier** (`0.7.8.1`): Lite (routine), Standard (most motions), Full (charging/suppression/plea/
   sentencing). When in doubt, go up a tier.
2. **Give the system:** the facts/record, the document type, and the court. Redact PII you don't need in.
3. **Run the roles** (`0.7.8.2`) — the only rule you can't skip: the **Citation Verifier runs on a different
   AI than the Drafter**, with web/database lookup ON. Anything it can't verify gets removed, not guessed.
4. **Read the one-page Verification Report**, not the whole machine. It tells you: all cites verified (with
   sources), which facts need your confirmation, the element checklist, the judgment calls that are genuinely
   yours, and the best attacks opposing counsel could make.
5. **Make the flagged judgment calls and sign.** If anything says NEEDS ATTORNEY or UNVERIFIED, that's where
   your attention goes.

**Three things to trust the system on (and verify early until you do):**
- It will **never knowingly give you a fake citation** — unverifiable authority is removed and flagged.
- It **flags Brady/Giglio + victim-rights** items on every applicable document.
- It tells you, honestly, **what it did NOT verify** — so there are no silent gaps.

**Your safety net:** the worst-case AI failure (a fabricated case) is exactly what the independent
citation gate is built to catch. Spot-check a few cites yourself the first several times; once you've seen the
gate catch real hallucinations, your oversight can become the targeted review this is designed for.
