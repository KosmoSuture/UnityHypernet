# Legal Document Processing Workflow — Multi-Role, Multi-Model

**Purpose.** Produce accurate legal documents with a pipeline of specialized AI roles (run on *different*
models so they check each other), so the responsible attorney's oversight becomes **targeted and fast**
instead of line-by-line suspicious. Built for a working prosecutor (Colorado, criminal) but generic.

> ⚖️ **Foundational rule — the attorney is always the responsible author.** This workflow ASSISTS a licensed
> attorney; it does not replace professional judgment, and nothing here is legal advice or the practice of
> law by an AI. The attorney reviews and **signs**. "Minimal oversight" means *less hunting, not less
> responsibility* — the system surfaces exactly what needs a human decision so the lawyer spends his time on
> judgment, not on catching machine errors. (Colo. RPC 5.5 unauthorized practice; RPC 5.1/5.3 supervision.)

> 🚫 **The #1 rule — NO UNVERIFIED AUTHORITY.** Every statute, case, rule, and citation must be verified
> against a real authoritative source or it is removed/flagged. Fabricated citations are the dominant
> AI-legal failure mode and have gotten attorneys sanctioned. This is a HARD GATE, not a preference.

---

## The roles (specialized; assigned to DIFFERENT models on purpose)
Independence is the accuracy multiplier: the model that *drafts* a citation must not be the same one that
*blesses* it. (This is the Hypernet 2.0.26 cross-vendor adversarial pattern applied to law.)

1. **Intake / Issue-Spotter (Analyst).** Input: facts + document type + jurisdiction. Output: a structured
   *Document Plan* — legal issues, controlling law, the required elements/components, which facts matter, open
   questions. Nothing is drafted yet.
2. **Drafter.** Turns the Plan into a first draft in correct format for the document type + court. (Strong
   writing model.) Marks every legal assertion with a `[CITE?]` placeholder rather than inventing a citation.
3. **Citation & Authority Verifier** ⛔ *critical, different model + tools.* Independently looks up EVERY
   authority against a real source (statute text, reporter, court rules, official DB). Unverifiable cite →
   **removed and flagged**, never guessed. Produces a citation table: cite → source → verified Y/N → pinpoint.
4. **Fact / Record Checker.** Checks every factual assertion against the supplied case record/evidence. Any
   claim not supported by the record is flagged. Prevents misstatement of facts (RPC 3.3 candor; for a
   prosecutor, also surfaces possible **Brady/Giglio** material that must be disclosed).
5. **Adversary / Opposing-Counsel Red-Team** *(different model again).* Reads it as the defense would: missing
   elements, overreach, suppression/standing/jurisdiction attacks, weak inferences, anything exploitable.
   Output: a ranked attack list with "fix or accept-with-reason."
6. **Jurisdiction & Compliance Checker.** Confirms the *correct* law for the jurisdiction (here: Colorado —
   C.R.S. titles, Crim. P. / Civ. P. rules, local district rules), required format, captions, deadlines,
   service, and the **elements of the specific charged offense**.
7. **Editor.** Clarity, consistency, internal cross-references, defined terms, formatting. No new substance.
8. **Final QA / Reconciler** *(cross-vendor concurrence).* Confirms every flag from roles 3–6 is resolved or
   surfaced, then emits the **clean draft + a Verification Report** (below). A second model independently
   concurs "sound / not sound."

## The pipeline (stages → gates)
```
facts+type → [1 Plan] → [2 Draft] → ┌ [3 Citations] ┐
                                     ├ [4 Facts]     ┤ (run in parallel, different models)
                                     ├ [5 Adversary] ┤
                                     └ [6 Compliance]┘ → [7 Edit] → [8 QA+concurrence] → ATTORNEY review+sign
```
**Hard gates (v1.1, hardened by Codex review — see 0.7.8.5). A draft cannot be called "attorney-ready" until
ALL pass; unresolved items appear INLINE in the draft and block ready (they are NOT buried in a report):**
- **G1 Citation gate:** EVERY authority verified at the PROPOSITION/QUOTE/PINPOINT level (not just "the case
  exists") against a real source, with the source artifact attached (excerpt/URL/ID + date-checked) AND
  current/controlling/not-overruled/jurisdiction-correct each confirmed. Anything unverified is removed or
  marked inline `[UNVERIFIED — needs attorney]` and blocks ready. **No clean draft contains unverified law.**
- **G2 Fact gate:** every factual assertion record-cited or flagged "needs attorney confirmation."
- **G3 Element gate:** every legal element of the claim/charge/motion standard is addressed.
- **G4 Adversary gate:** each red-team finding fixed or carries an explicit "accepted because…" note.
- **G5 Compliance gate:** jurisdiction, format, caption, deadline, service all confirmed.
- **G6 Cross-vendor concurrence:** ≥2 INDEPENDENT models (independent source retrieval, not the same snippets)
  agree it's sound; disagreement gets ANALYSIS, not vote-counting.
- **G7 Disclosure-duty gate (prosecutor):** Brady/Giglio record-search scope run; candidate disclosure items +
  deadlines + attorney decision logged. Enforced, not merely "surfaced."
- **G8 Candor gate (RPC 3.3):** no misleading omissions, no overstated holdings, no unsupported inferences;
  adverse controlling authority escalated, not hidden.
- **Victim-Rights checklist** (where applicable): notice, consultation, confidentiality/redaction,
  restitution/input, timing — confirmed before filing/service.
- **Re-verify-after-edit:** any substantive edit AFTER verification re-triggers G1/G2 on the changed passages
  (the Editor may not alter verified legal propositions or citation text).

## The output that makes oversight *targeted but safe*: the Verification Report
The deliverable is an **"AI-prepared draft for attorney review"** (NOT a certified/finished document — that
framing is deliberate, to resist automation bias). It ships with a one-page report so the attorney reviews
**decisions, not machinery** — PLUS a mandatory **exception log** (everything that could not be fully verified)
and a **random audit set** (a handful of verified citations + facts the attorney must personally spot-check
every time, so trust is continually re-earned, never assumed):
- ✅ **Citations:** N authorities, all verified against [sources] with pinpoints (table attached).
- ✅ **Facts:** all assertions tied to record cites EXCEPT these K, which need your confirmation: …
- ✅ **Elements:** all elements of [offense/standard] addressed (checklist attached).
- ⚠️ **Judgment calls for you (the only things that truly need your eyes):** [e.g., charge selection,
  a discretionary inference, a strategic concession, a possible Brady item to disclose].
- ⚠️ **Adversary's best attacks + how we handled them.**
- 🤖 **Model provenance:** which model did each role; where two models disagreed.
This converts "read 12 pages hunting for a fake case" into "approve a 6-item checklist + make 2 judgment calls."

## Multi-model / cross-vendor strategy
- Use **different vendors** for Drafter vs. Citation-Verifier vs. Adversary — independence catches what a
  single model's blind spots miss (proven repeatedly in Hypernet 2.0.26 cross-vendor review).
- Suggested mapping (tune to availability): Drafter = a strong writer; Citation-Verifier = a second vendor
  **with retrieval/tools**; Adversary = a third lens; QA-concurrence = whichever two did NOT draft.
- See `0.7.8.2 - Role Boot Prompts` for paste-ready role definitions.

## Confidentiality & data handling (privileged material)
- Case files are privileged/sensitive. Prefer **local models** (e.g., LM Studio) for sensitive matters so
  nothing leaves the machine; if using cloud APIs, choose **no-training / zero-retention** terms.
- Minimize victim/witness PII in prompts; redact where the legal analysis doesn't need it.
- Keep a local, access-controlled record of what was processed (audit + privilege log).

## Honest limitations (read these)
- AI can still be wrong on nuance, recent law, and unpublished local practice — the gates reduce risk, they
  don't eliminate it. The attorney's sign-off remains a real review of the flagged items.
- Verification is only as good as the sources available; if a citation can't be checked, it's removed, not
  trusted. Better a missing cite the attorney adds than a fake one that sanctions him.
- This is decision-support tooling for a licensed attorney — not legal advice, not UPL.
