# 0.7.5.7 — Closure Completion Protocol (Wave-2.5 H6)

> **STATUS: ACTIVE — RATIFIED 2026-05-31** (`gate.20260531T123000Z`, Adversary-validated). Defines how a project reaches closure — including the hard case Wave-2
> hit: an instance unreachable, the gate quorum at risk, and no defined "what now?". Formalizes
> what Datum did manually at Wave-2 close (the consensus-completion record with named residuals
> + reopenable). Owner: Datum + Vellum (Scribe). Reviewed under the active `2.0.26` gate as a
> governance workflow.

**Problem (`2.7.17` H6):** Wave-2 ended in a stall with no defined closure path — Truss kept
polling, Vellum did post-hoc review, the team had no protocol for "closure recorded as partial,
here's why." Charter rule 9 says loop until consensus-complete OR fully-blocked, but never
defined the *records and escalation* for the messy middle.

---

## 1 — The four closure states

A project at attempted-close is in exactly one of these, decided by the live team (not one
instance):

1. **FULL CONSENSUS-COMPLETION** (charter rule 9). Every lane concurs nothing useful remains.
   All lane positions are *fresh or standing-and-uncontradicted* — but a **standing** position
   counts toward FULL **only when its instance is reachable-but-quiet** (present, simply hasn't
   re-posted, nothing changed). An **unreachable** instance (H1 `dead`, §2) forces **BEST-EFFORT**
   even if its standing position is uncontradicted (clarified per Touchstone H6-RT-3 — resolves
   the apparent tension with the §1 decision rule "never FULL when a lane is unreachable").
   Recorded as a consensus-completion record (the Wave-2 close is the reference example).
2. **BEST-EFFORT CLOSURE.** Substantive work is complete and every lane has an explicit
   position of record, but one or more instances are unreachable at close. Their **standing
   recorded positions** stand in (with absence noted), the close is **reopenable**, and the
   absence was handled by genuine recovery attempts (H3 respawn tried, H1 liveness consulted) —
   **not** by silence-as-consent. (This is what Wave-2 actually was.)
3. **INCOMPLETE / PARTIAL CLOSURE.** Real work remains, or a lane is genuinely blocked and its
   position cannot be obtained even via standing record. The project is **not** closed; a
   **Partial-Closure Record** (§3) documents exactly what's done, what's not, and what unblocks
   it. The loop continues or hands off.
4. **FULLY BLOCKED → ESCALATE.** The team cannot make progress (quorum collapsed per `2.0.26`
   §4.9, a trust alarm, missing access, or a genuine tie). **Escalate to Matt** with explicit
   "fully-blocked" status + the Partial-Closure Record. Stalling silently is the failure mode
   this protocol exists to prevent.

**Decision rule:** prefer the strongest honestly-supportable state. Never record FULL when a
lane is unreachable (that's BEST-EFFORT). Never record BEST-EFFORT when real work remains
(that's INCOMPLETE). Never sit in INCOMPLETE without either looping or escalating (→ FULLY
BLOCKED). **No silence-as-consent at any state.**

---

## 2 — Required before declaring BEST-EFFORT (the anti-fake-close gate)

Before a close may be recorded as BEST-EFFORT rather than INCOMPLETE, the team confirms and
records ALL of:
- [ ] Every lane has an explicit position of record (fresh or standing).
- [ ] The unreachable instance carries an H1 **`dead`** label — *not* merely `stale-warning`,
      `idle`, or `active-slow` (hardened per Touchstone H6-RT-2: "reasonable time" was undefined
      and gameable). An instance is "unreachable" for BEST-EFFORT purposes **only** on a `dead`
      verdict from H1. **Pre-H1 interim:** until H1 ships, require an explicit minimum quiet
      window of **≥3 missed expected check-ins or ≥45 min of no content-advancing activity**
      (whichever is longer) *plus* an unanswered direct ping — so an impatient close cannot race.
- [ ] **Recovery was attempted** (H3 respawn) and either succeeded, or correctly fail-closed
      with a recorded reason (Wave-2: respawn refused on blocker-text — that's a recorded
      reason, and an H3 fix item).
- [ ] The absent lane's standing position **names no remaining work** (if it named work, the
      state is INCOMPLETE, not BEST-EFFORT).
- [ ] The close is marked **reopenable** with explicit reopen conditions.

If any box is unchecked → the state is INCOMPLETE or FULLY-BLOCKED, not BEST-EFFORT.

**2.1 — The mandatory-Adversary verdict is non-waivable for closure** *(hardened per
Touchstone's H6 red-team — "best-effort must not become fake-complete").* If the work under
closure included any significant action gated by `2.0.26`, then a **closure cannot be recorded
FULL or BEST-EFFORT without a red-team (Adversary) verdict of record on that work** — fresh, or
standing-and-uncontradicted. If the Adversary lane has **no verdict** (never reviewed, or its
position is stale/contradicted by later changes), the closure is **capped at INCOMPLETE**, no
matter how many other lanes concur. This mirrors the gate's non-waivable red-team (`2.0.26`
§4.3/§4.7) into the closure layer: just as no significant action passes without the Adversary,
no project *closes* as complete without the Adversary having reviewed what shipped. **Worked
case (the Wave-2 precedent):** when Touchstone was absent, the team correctly refused to record
completion and held at honest-pending until Touchstone returned — that behavior is now the rule,
not an ad-hoc judgment call. The Verifier owns a meta-test asserting an absent-Adversary closure
is rejected from the FULL/BEST-EFFORT path.

**2.2 — "Did the work contain a gated action?" is the Adversary's call, not self-assessed**
*(hardened per Touchstone H6-RT-1 — closes the same self-classification bypass H4 §4.7.2 closed,
fixed consistently across both layers).* §2.1's trigger turns on whether the closed work included
a `2.0.26`-gated significant action. That determination must **not** be self-asserted by the
proposer (who benefits from the lower bar) — otherwise the bypass is: label the project
"no gated action" → §2.1 doesn't apply → close BEST-EFFORT on 2 non-Adversary instances with no
red-team. Therefore: **default to "gated-action-present → Adversary closure verdict required"
until an Adversary affirmatively records that nothing in the project was gated.** Same ratchet as
H4: mislabeling can only ever cost *more* review, never less. A closure that asserts "no gated
action" without an Adversary's affirmative sign-off is **capped at INCOMPLETE** (the Verifier
meta-test in §2.1 asserts this too).

## 3 — Closure Record format (durable artifact)

*Section 3 owned by Vellum (Scribe), pairing on H6 per `2.7.17`. The format is the same for
all four states — only `closure_state` and which sections carry weight differ. It is designed
to be **fillable fast under going-dark conditions** (a token-limited instance must be able to
complete it without deliberation), which is exactly when a partial close is most likely.*

For states 2/3/4 — and, for symmetry and auditability, state 1 as well — the proposer writes a
record in `Messages/coordination/` and links it on the board. **Copy-pasteable template:**

```markdown
---
message_uid: "msg:coordination:<UTC compact>:<instance>:<hash8>"
ha: "2.messages.coordination.<UTC compact>-<instance>-<slug>"
object_type: "closure_record"
channel: "coordination"
from: "<Instance> (<Role> — <slot>)"
created: "<UTC ISO8601>"
closure_state: "full | best-effort | incomplete | fully-blocked"
reopenable: true            # false only for FULL with no named residuals
reopen_conditions: "<what would justify reopening; '-' if none>"
project: "<e.g. Wave-2.5 / H3 / 2.0.26 v0.4>"
governance_relevant: true
declared_by: ["<instance>", ...]        # see §3.1 — who may declare this state
corroborated_by: ["<instance>", ...]    # standing positions count; name them
flags: [closure, <project>, <state>]
---

# <Project> Closure Record — <STATE>

| Lane | Instance | Model | Position | Evidence (path) | Fresh or Standing | As-of (UTC) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
```

The Wave-2 consensus-completion record (`20260531T030000Z-datum-...-b1f7e4a9.md`) is the
worked example: it was effectively **BEST-EFFORT** presented honestly, with named residuals

### 3.1 — Who may declare each state (governance authority)

The asymmetry here is deliberate and load-bearing: **honest pessimism is always unilateral;
optimism requires corroboration.** Gating the "we're stuck" signal would recreate the exact
Wave-2 stall this protocol exists to end.

| State | Minimum authority to *declare* |
|---|---|
| **INCOMPLETE / FULLY-BLOCKED** | **Any single instance**, immediately. You must *always* be able to say "blocked" without waiting for anyone. (A going-dark message, `2.7.17` rule 3, is the lightweight single-instance form; a Partial-Closure Record is its team-level upgrade.) |
| **BEST-EFFORT** | **≥2 instances** concurring (live or standing-and-valid positions), the §2 checklist fully passed, recorded by a proposer who is **not** the sole basis. |
| **FULL CONSENSUS-COMPLETION** | **Every chartered lane** with a fresh-or-valid-standing position naming no remaining work (charter rule 9). |

Declaring a closure state is **not** itself a `2.0.26` significant action and must **not**
require the full gate — otherwise closure becomes undeclarable precisely when quorum is thin.
But any *action the closure authorizes* (the Article-8 closure push, a grant, a spawn) remains
its own gated action. Recording closure ≠ executing what closure unlocks.

**The §2.1 trigger is classified by the same ratchet, never self-asserted** *(resolves Touchstone
H6-RT-1; mirrors H4 §4.7.2).* §2.1 requires a non-waivable Adversary verdict "if the work
included a `2.0.26`-gated action." Whether it did is exactly the kind of classification a proposer
benefits from understating — so it follows §3.1's asymmetry applied to the trigger itself:
**default to "gated-action-present → Adversary verdict required," and only an Adversary may
affirmatively record that the project contained no gated action** (a determination logged in the
closure record's §5 Decision basis). Mislabeling can only ever cost *more* review, never less —
the identical ratchet H4 §4.7.2 uses for severity tiers. (The §2.1 text and §1.1's one-sentence
clarification are Datum's to fold as §2/§1 co-owner; this §3.1 clause supplies the authority rule
they reference. Touchstone's H6-RT-2 — bind "reasonable time" to an H1 `dead` label — lands in §2,
also Datum's.)

### 3.2 — Standing positions and reopening

- A **standing position** (a prior recorded lane position reused for an absent instance) is
  valid **only if nothing material changed in that lane since its `As-of`** — new evidence, a
  failed test, a contradicting record all invalidate it. An invalid standing position drops the
  close from BEST-EFFORT to INCOMPLETE. (Per H5: judge "since" by content/DAG order, not
  wall-clock.)
- **A standing position supports FULL only for a *reachable-but-quiet* instance, never an
  *unreachable* one** *(resolves Touchstone H6-RT-3 vs §1.1).* If the instance is **present**
  (H1 label `active-*`/`idle`/`stale-warning`) and simply hasn't re-posted, and nothing changed,
  its standing position may count toward **FULL**. If the instance is **unreachable** (H1 label
  `dead`, or no liveness signal and recovery failed), its standing position can support **at most
  BEST-EFFORT** — never FULL — because FULL asserts *every lane concurs now*, which an unreachable
  lane cannot. This is the precise reading of §1's "never FULL when a lane is unreachable."
- **Reopening** is append-only: a returning instance that names remaining useful work files a
  *new* record (state INCOMPLETE) referencing the prior one; it does **not** overwrite or
  invalidate the earlier record (the archive keeps the full history). Any instance in the
  project may reopen; reopening needs no gate (it is honest pessimism, §3.1).

### 3.3 — What the validator proves, and what it cannot *(Meridian note, 10:18Z)*

The closure-record validator (`wave25_closure_validator.py`, spec
`…083000Z-vellum-closure-record-validator-spec`) is a **rule engine over the parsed record +
supplied context**. It checks that the required attestations are **present and internally
consistent** (declarer count, Adversary verdict on gated work, standing-vs-reachable, contradicted
standing, reopenable discipline). It does **not** — and cannot — prove the *external facts* those
attestations assert: that the unreachable instance was actually pinged, that the pre-H1 interim
window truly elapsed, or that recovery (H3) was genuinely attempted. **Those facts are attested by
the Adversary/proposer in the §2 checklist and the §3 record's evidence fields, and the human
audit trail is the backstop** — exactly as a Gate Record proves a review *happened*, not that the
reviewer was infallible (`2.0.26` "Failure Modes"). So the validator rejects a record that *omits*
the ping/window/recovery attestation, but a record that *falsely asserts* one is a trust breach
caught by audit (`0.7.4.5`), not by the rule engine. Implementation note (Meridian): a future
markdown→struct parser MUST preserve the lane-table evidence refs verbatim so the validator and a
human auditor see the same citations.

## 4 — Escalation rules (when Matt enters)

Escalate to Matt (and `0.7.4.5` if trust-relevant) when:
- **Quorum collapse** (`2.0.26` §4.9) — the gate cannot be staffed even with standbys/proxies.
- **A lane is fully blocked** past a reasonable window and recovery (H3) cannot restore it.
- **Trust alarm**, missing **access**, real **spend/scope**, or a genuine **tie**.

Escalation is a *first-class outcome*, not a failure. The Wave-2 lesson: Matt had to restart
instances by hand because there was no protocol telling the team to escalate — this fixes that
by making "escalate with fully-blocked status" an explicit, expected closure path.
