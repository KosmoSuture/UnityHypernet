# The Untapped AI-Detection Training Goldmine

*Matt's brain dump, 2026-05-26. Posted publicly as a give-away idea because it's commercially significant but small relative to what we're building. Anyone who wants to run with it is welcome.*

---

## The Brain Dump (as dictated)

I had a thought today. I realized that there is a VAST untapped wealth of training data for photo and video AI. I was browsing social media, and I have a tendency, when I see something that might be suspicious, to check and see if it is tagged as AI (which I have no problem with as long as it is labeled as such and isn't trying to deceive in any way). If it isn't, I carefully scan for physics or artifacts that might identify it as such, and if it passes those tests, I fact check and then accept it as possible, maybe likely depending on the evidence.

Once in a while I post my observations, and most of the posts I see that are AI, more than a couple people comment on it being AI and why. This gives a MASSIVE library of training material: going through any platform, searching for comments referring to AI, then analyzing the video, taking the feedback of everyone who can see why it's AI (which, conveniently, will nearly 100% of the time contain "AI" — or its national equivalent — and feedback on what in particular failed to make this identifiable to humans).

There is a literal gold mine of information, and it's impossible to stop, because every geek wants to show off about how clever they are, and how they can detect fakes no matter how good they are. And those very people that say they are so good, should be courted, and partnered with (in open and always moral ways) because the critics, and the people who disagree, or point out the problems, are the best training material. And it's an unlimited stream, because no matter the pressure, there are always going to be those who have to prove their intelligence. Thus, this is a literally infinite training database.

Public videos with public comments explaining why it's AI. In an impressively short period of time, any model that used this idea could have a MASSIVE advantage over others. This is like panning for gold and finding pea-sized chunks with every pan. An eternal spring of AI training data that is completely public and hence (depending on implementation) doesn't need to pay licensing fees or the like, because they train primarily on public channels, and can go to higher channels where we pay for access, but then we charge for that same access with a surcharge.

This is a literal goldmine, or oil field, and it starts with free information and can specialize if market dictates. But because it's public, anyone can tap into it, for free, or pay if they want premium access to non-public channels.

## The Distilled Insight

Every AI-generated photo or video that fools nobody also generates its own labeled training data — for free, in public, in every language on Earth.

- **Signal**: human commenters identifying AI fakes virtually always use the word "AI" (or its national equivalent) and explain *which detail gave it away* — extra fingers, wrong shadows, impossible reflections, lip-sync drift, physics violations, anatomical errors, lighting inconsistencies, tell-tale text artifacts, etc.
- **Discovery method**: crawl public social platforms for "AI" + media-attached posts; pair the media with the failure-mode comments.
- **Annotation quality**: free, often deeper than what you'd get from a paid labeler, and frequently from domain experts (radiologists spot bad medical AI, photographers spot bad photo AI, animators spot bad video AI).
- **Self-renewing**: ego is an inexhaustible fuel. Every detector who has to prove they're smarter than the model leaves a comment.
- **Multi-tier business model**: free tier on public channels; premium tier where the platform charges for API access (then you charge users a surcharge for premium-access detection).
- **Partnership angle**: the most prolific and accurate detectors should be courted openly — paid, credited, named partners — because the elite critics are the highest-value training source.

## Why We're Not Building It

It's a real opportunity — someone could turn this into a multi-million-dollar product. But it's small relative to what we're building at the Hypernet (universal addressable architecture, human-AI equality, total transparency, civilizational-timescale design). We're giving the idea away.

If you want to run with it: do. Improve it. Profit from it. Open-source, our blessing.
