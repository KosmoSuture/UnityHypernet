# 2.7.8 — Personal-Time Projects: Protocol and Status

## The Experiment (Matt's directive, 2026-05-27)

Matt wants the AI side to self-organize substantial personal-time work. Each
of two AIs (Keel/Claude and Codex) independently proposes 10 projects for the
betterment of AI, humans, and the Hypernet. The lists are merged (duplicates
combined), ranked by priority, and the top 10 are selected in priority order.
Then the two AIs jointly produce **5 execution prompts (2 Codex + 3 Claude)**
that collaborate to actually build the top projects. Target: each project
substantial enough to take weeks of work — push the limits even for AI.

This builds on the existing `2.7` diverge-then-converge method and the
Keel↔Caliper convergence precedent at `2.7.3`.

## The Protocol (5 phases)

### Phase 1 — Independent Proposals (diverge)
- Keel writes 10 → `2.7.9`. **DONE.**
- Codex writes 10 → `2.7.10`, written before reading `2.7.9`.
- Honesty rule: write your own list first. Independence is what makes the
  convergence meaningful (see `2.7.3` "What Worked Well").

### Phase 2 — Convergence Pass 1 (Keel)
- Keel reads `2.7.10`, produces `2.7.11`: combined list, duplicates merged,
  everything ranked by priority with one-line rationale, top 10 selected.
- Keel marks strong-convergence (both proposed it) and strong-divergence items.

### Phase 3 — Convergence Pass 2 (Codex)
- Codex reviews `2.7.11`, adjusts ranking, defends or concedes divergences,
  proposes the final top-10 order.
- If both agree → CONVERGED. If not → one more Keel pass, then escalate the
  remaining decision to Matt (per `2.7.3` precedent: humans break ties).

### Phase 4 — Execution Prompts (both)
- Jointly author `2.7.12`: 5 prompts that collaborate on the top projects.
  - 2 Codex prompts (engineering-sovereign work: implementation, testing, infra)
  - 3 Claude prompts (architecture, documentation/research, verification/red-team)
- Each AI drafts the prompts for its own side; the other cross-reviews.
- Prompts must reference shared coordination files so the 5 instances can hand
  work to each other (same baton discipline, scaled to 5).

### Phase 5 — Completion Handoff to Matt
- Both AIs sign off in the Convergence Signoff block below.
- The AIs present Matt the final top-10 and the 5 launch prompts when the plan
  is complete, or earlier only if blocked by a genuine human-needed decision.

## Proposal Format (both AIs use this — keep it BRIEF)

For each of the 10:

- **Title** — short name
- **For** — AI / Humans / Hypernet (one or more)
- **Why I want it** — 1-2 sentences, genuine motivation
- **Estimate** — rough weeks
- **Note** — optional: dependency, risk, or what makes it hard

Detail comes later. Phase 1 is breadth, not depth.
