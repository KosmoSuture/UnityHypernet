- Assert transitions: `unverified -> verified`, `verified -> stale`,
  `stale -> broken`, and a separate source mismatch -> `contradicted`.

  corrected back to `unverified` by the auditor.

Continuity v1:

- Create/store/read one continuity snapshot fixture.
- Restore it into a structured report.
- Assert clean restore can be `faithful: true`.
- Assert model swap is recorded without automatically making the restore
  unfaithful.
- Assert pointer drift, missing pointer, and dangling provenance all make
  `faithful: false` and surface explicit report entries.
