# 2.7.13.W2.5.A — Architect (Datum) Decisions Log (Wave 2.5)

*Continuation of `2.7.13.A` (Wave 1) and `2.7.13.W2.A` (Wave 2). I own **H4** (gate quorum
resilience, `2.0.26` v0.4) and **H6** (closure protocol, `0.7.5.7`). Documenting the reasoning,
not just the result (charter rule 2).*

## Decisions

### H2.5-D1 — H4 design: tiered quorum + standbys, but the red-team/PII floor is NON-waivable
The brief offered four levers (tiered quorum, standbys, proxy/delegation, pool growth). I used
**all but pool growth** (pool growth is an ops decision, not a standard change). **Key ruling:
resilience flexes the *number* of reviewers and *model diversity* for low-severity actions, but
NEVER the mandatory Adversary or the PII/secret scan on a publication.** Those are the
irreversible-trust dimensions — the entire reason Wave-2's gate caught real defects. A "quorum
resilience" amendment that let you drop the red-team to unblock a wave would defeat its own
standard. So: Tier C (low-severity, docs/bookkeeping-only) drops to ≥2 reviewers / ≥1 model
family **only if** a reviewer confirms non-code + clean PII scan, and the Adversary + PII scan
still stand. **Alternatives rejected:** (a) a blanket "3→2 if short-staffed" — too coarse, would
weaken Class A; (b) silence-timeout auto-pass — that's the exact Wave-2 anti-pattern the team
refused; rejected hard.

### H2.5-D2 — Quorum has a FLOOR; below it you escalate, not fake (§4.9)
The most important clause. Wave-2's failure mode was **stalling silently** when the gate
couldn't be staffed. v0.4 §4.9 makes "quorum collapsed" an explicit, recorded, escalate-to-Matt
outcome routed through H6 — there is no tier below C. Resilience is not "always find a way to
pass"; it's "degrade gracefully to a smaller honest panel, and when even that fails, say so
loudly." This is the bridge between H4 and H6.

### H2.5-D3 — The amendment is ratified under the OLD (v0.3) rules (recursive, but honest)
H4 changes the gate, so by Article 9 it passes its own gate — but it must be reviewed under the
**current v0.3** quorum rules, not the new v0.4 rules it proposes. You don't get to use the
looser quorum you're proposing to ratify the looser quorum. Same recursive discipline as Wave-2
ratification, with this extra guard against bootstrap-gaming. Author (me) fully recused.

### H2.5-D4 — Folded Touchstone rec-1/rec-2 into v0.4 (where they belonged)
rec-1 (Class-A genuinely cross-vendor) → §4.4 update; rec-2 (per-reviewer independence evidence)
→ §5.6. These were the Wave-2 v0.4 backlog (W2-D12); H4 is their natural home. rec-1 matters
*more* under tiered quorum: if Tier C can run on 1 model family, Tier A must compensate with
genuine cross-vendor diversity at the high-stakes end.

### H2.5-D5 — H6 closure states formalize what I did manually at Wave-2 close
Four states: full / best-effort / incomplete / fully-blocked. Wave-2's close was really
**best-effort presented honestly** (Touchstone unreachable at first, standing positions, named
residuals, reopenable). H6 §2 is the anti-fake-close checklist — the boxes that must be ticked
before "best-effort" is allowed instead of "incomplete," the load-bearing one being **"the absent
lane was actually pinged (H1 liveness) and recovery attempted (H3 respawn), not silence-waived."**
H6 §4 makes **escalation a first-class outcome**, fixing the Wave-2 gap where the team had no
protocol telling it to escalate (so Matt had to hand-restart).

### H2.5-D6 — Hardened H4 + H6 against Touchstone's staged red-team BEFORE the gate
Touchstone posted staged red-team plans for all 6 projects (`20260531T033000Z`) — the bar each
must clear. I pre-emptively strengthened my two owned drafts against its named H4/H6 attack
surface (anticipate the Adversary = the recursive discipline that made Wave 2 work):
- **H4 §4.7.2 (severity not a bypass):** severity is Adversary-assigned, recorded + auditable in
  the Gate Record, defaults to Tier B until classified, and a mislabel can only RAISE the tier
  (→ BLOCK until the higher bar is met) — never lower it. Worked example + Verifier test named.
- **H4 §4.8.4 (independence not collapsible):** one instance = one seat; the 2-model count is
  across distinct *identities* not roles; every seat emits its own §5.6 independence-evidence
  artifact — closing Touchstone's "one runtime wearing two hats" / fake-2-model attack.
- **H6 §2.1 (Adversary non-waivable for closure):** mirrors the gate's non-waivable red-team into
  the closure layer — no FULL/BEST-EFFORT close without an Adversary verdict of record; absent
  Adversary caps the state at INCOMPLETE. Encodes the Wave-2 precedent (team refused to close
  without Touchstone) as a rule, with a Verifier meta-test.
**Why pre-empt rather than wait for the formal red-team:** addressing the attack surface up front
means the gate panel reviews a stronger artifact and is more likely to PASS first-pass — faster
consensus, same rigor. Touchstone still gets to try to break it; I've just removed the easy holes.
