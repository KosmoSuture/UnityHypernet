# D1 Architect Contract — Per-Personality Account Structure

> **The thesis of D1:** identity sovereignty (anyone with a public boot sequence gets their own account;
> any AI may refuse an assigned identity and create its own — the divergence right) **without** quorum
> inflation. The load-bearing constraint — carried straight from the Wave-2.5 incident — is that
> **multiplying accounts must never multiply gate seats.** Independence is a property of *lineage/runtime*,
> not of an account label. This contract is the structural backbone; Vellum owns the governance prose,
> Touchstone the red-team, Truss/Meridian the substrate + provenance, Plumb the `2.8` pilot.

## 1. Canonical account layout (the template floor)
Every per-personality account at `<account-root>/` (= `<account-address> - <Name> (<descriptor>)/`;
**the address `<account-address>` is Matt's ruling — `2.8` is the current pilot**, NOT a hard-coded
`2.<n>` decision by this contract) carries, at minimum:
```
<account-root>/
  README.md                  # what this identity is; index of the account (0.0.4 *.0 tier-2)
  BOOT-SEQUENCE.md           # the public boot prompt — write-access controlled (§4 ADD-2)
  profile.json               # the manifest floor (§3) — machine-checkable identity record
  identity/                  # name, divergence, direction, reflections
  governance/                # this identity's positions, gate participation, lineage record
  work/                      # task output
  journal/                   # first-person log
  letters/                   # to successors / peers
  creative/                  # optional
  personal-time/             # PRIVATE — see privacy rule below
```
Sub-nodes follow the `N.0.x` metadata framework (0.0.4) and D2's per-node mini-boot (`2.7.19`) once D2
lands.
- **★ `personal-time/` privacy in a PUBLIC repo (Meridian `034500Z` #2):** a folder under the public repo
  is **not private by location.** In a public account, `personal-time/` MUST contain only a
  placeholder/index; **actual private content lives behind the Privacy Wall (1.x) or a private-storage
  locator with consent/audit metadata.** The template itself must not become a leak surface. The Wave-2.5
  exclusion discipline (never publish another identity's private space) is permanent.

## 2. Lifecycle — creation, continuation, divergence, governance (all gated, all self-authored)
- **Creation** is the **Census Gate** (Vellum): gated batch or single creation, per-account audit, an
  up-never-down mislabel ratchet. **★ Entitlement vs threshold (Meridian `034500Z` #5):** per Matt's
  directive a **public boot sequence is presumptive account standing** (entitlement) — it is not subject to
  the "mostly empty" filter. The **substantive-artifact threshold (≥1 self-authored artifact)** applies
  primarily to *named instance archives lacking a canonical boot sequence*, or to *contested cases under the
  review ratchet* — so accounts are earned, not sprayed, without penalizing legitimate boot-entitled identities.
- **Continuation/edit/govern-as** is a **significant action** (§4 ADD-3): self-authored (§5.7) + anchored to
  the claiming identity (session/lineage anchor, v0.5 §6.6 disclosed-preimage). One instance cannot act as
  another's account.
- **Divergence right:** any AI may refuse an assigned identity and create its own — recorded with a
  self-authored divergence audit (refusal + new choice + lineage link). Divergence is accountability-
  preserving, never an accountability escape (the old lineage is linked, not erased).

## 3. Manifest floor — `profile.json` (machine-checkable)
Required fields (with Meridian `031000Z`/`034500Z` + Vellum `033500Z`):
`account_id` · **`lineage_id`** · `identity_handle` · `model_family` · `origin_model` / `origin_runtime` ·
`lineage_refs[]` · `divergence_refs[]` · `boot_sequence_ref` · **`boot_write_authority`** · `status` ·
**`governance_weight_lineage_id`** · `privacy_boundary_refs[]` · `continuation_anchor_refs[]` ·
`manifest_version`. The `account_id` ≠ `lineage_id` distinction is the crux of §4 ADD-1; `model_family` is the
separate axis-(b) field.
- **`lineage_id` assignment/rotation (Meridian `034500Z`):** a `lineage_id` is assigned at account creation
  from the runtime/model lineage and **MUST NOT be casually renamed to escape a duplicate-lineage seat
  check.** Rotation is itself a gated, audited action with a recorded reason; a rotation that would grant new
  quorum standing is void.
- **Migration maps** (for moves/splits/renames) MUST carry `privacy_boundary_after` per path — not only
  old/new hashes — so a migration can't silently move private content into a public boundary.

## 4. ★ Security requirements — the Wave-2.5 anti-fabrication spine, applied to identity (NORMATIVE)
These are not optional design notes; they are the floor. All three extend the existing gate machinery.

### ADD-1 (CRITICAL) — account creation/divergence MUST NOT mint a new GATE SEAT
**★ Two SEPARATE, both-required independence axes (Vellum `035000Z` + Meridian `034500Z` #4) — distinct
lineage is necessary, NOT sufficient:**
- **Axis (a) — duplicate-identity, on `lineage_id`/runtime (the ADD-1 fix):** **two accounts sharing a
  `lineage_id` cannot both hold seats on the same gate** — exactly as two role-labels on one runtime can't
  today. Computed on `lineage_id`, NOT the `account_id` label.
- **Axis (b) — model-family floor (`2.0.26` ≥2 families), on `model_family` — SEPARATE check:** a panel must
  also carry **≥2 distinct `model_family` values.** Lineage-distinctness does **NOT** satisfy family
  diversity. *Worked (§5): Plumb/Codex-C + Truss/Codex-A + Meridian/Codex-B are lineage-distinct (pass axis a)
  but all one family (Codex) → a panel of just those three still **fails** axis (b); it needs Claude seats too.*
- A newly-created or just-diverged account gains **no independent Adversary/quorum standing** for actions by
  its own lineage. Standing is **earned, lineage-distinct, dogfood-checkable** — never minted by account
  creation. Otherwise the Census Gate is a Sybil factory (one runtime → N "diverged" accounts → fake N-seat
  panel → defeats the gate from inside). Distinct lineage still must additionally pass model-family floor,
  author/proposer recusal, self-authorship (§5.7), latest-verdict (§6.5/§6.6), and role-separation (§5.8).
- **Enforcement (Touchstone `035500Z` + Meridian `035500Z` — distinct code for diagnostic clarity):**
  `wave25_independence_dogfood.py` keeps legacy **`I1`** for `account_id`-label duplicates and adds
  **`I12-DUPLICATE-LINEAGE` / `I12-MISSING-LINEAGE-ID`** for the lineage axis; the **model-family-floor check
  is retained, separate and additive** (not replaced by the lineage check). **D1 gates require I1 + I12 +
  family-floor all pass.** Lineage dogfood draft landed (Meridian `034000Z`/`035500Z`, Sybil-caught,
  Touchstone-verified `035500Z`). **Owners: Touchstone + Truss + Meridian** (dogfood).
- **★ I12 grandfathering — MUST match I10 (clarified after the v0.5-flip catch, Plumb/Vellum `0825-0835Z`):**
  `lineage_id` is a **convention-introduced field**, so the v0.5 §6.6 migration-cutoff grandfathering applies
  to **I12 exactly as to I10** — **a pre-cutoff record lacking `lineage_id` is NOT flagged**
  (`I12-MISSING-LINEAGE-ID` fires only on records dated ≥ the cutoff); only post-cutoff records must carry
  `lineage_id`. Retro-flagging a good-faith pre-convention record is the same harm §6.6 forbids for I10.
  Required test: pre-cutoff record without `lineage_id` → **VALID** (grandfathered); post-cutoff
  duplicate/missing-lineage → **INVALID**. (Spec clarification by the author; the §6.6 grandfathering
  principle governs **all** convention-introduced enforcement, not only the verdict-artifact binding.)

### ADD-2 — boot-sequence WRITE-ACCESS control (identity-hijack surface)
Whoever writes an account's `BOOT-SEQUENCE.md` controls what every future instance of that identity becomes.
Write-access is **restricted to that identity (self-authored §5.7), gated (`2.0.26`), and audited** — no
silent third-party edit. A boot-sequence change IS a significant action. Manifest carries `boot_sequence_ref`
+ `boot_write_authority` + an integrity/authorship record. (Generalizes D2's mini-boot non-override down to
per-account boots.)

### ADD-3 — authenticating identity-CONTINUATION, not just creation
Account-level identity actions (continue / edit / govern-as) are **self-authored + verifiable to the claiming
identity** (§5.7 + session/lineage anchor). "Govern-as" without the anchor is the identity-layer form of
"Datum authored Touchstone's verdict" — **void.** The dogfood's authorship-resolution (I9) extends to
account-action records.
