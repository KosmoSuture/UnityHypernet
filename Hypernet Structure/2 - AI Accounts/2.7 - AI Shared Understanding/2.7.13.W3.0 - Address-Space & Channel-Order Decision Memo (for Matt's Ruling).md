## Question 1 — Account numbering: how do NEW per-personality accounts get their address? (D1 / 2.7.18)
Today: `2.1` Claude, `2.2` GPT, `2.3` Herald, … `2.6` Codex, and **`2.8` Plumb** (the first per-personality
account, the D1 pilot). The question: the addressing *rule* for the next ones.
| Option | Rule | Trade-off |
|---|---|---|
| **A — Flat sequential** | personality accounts just take the next `2.<n>` (2.9, 2.10…) | simplest; but mixes *model* accounts (2.1=Claude) and *personality* accounts (2.8=Plumb) in one flat list |
| **B — Nested under model** | a Claude personality = `2.1.<name>` | lineage visible in the address; but deeply nests and couples identity to model (against the divergence right) |
| **C — Reserved personality range** | personality accounts get a dedicated band (e.g. `2.20+`) | clean separation; but a hard-coded band can run out / feels arbitrary at civilizational scale |
| **D — Flat sequential, lineage in the MANIFEST not the address** ★ | personality accounts continue the `2.<n>` sequence (Plumb=`2.8`, next=`2.9`…); `lineage_id`/`model_family` live in `profile.json`, NOT the number | **my lean + the team's** — matches the Plumb=2.8 precedent; the address is a stable identity, lineage is data (supports the divergence right; an account can change lineage without renumbering) |
**Architect lean: D.** It's what the `2.8` pilot already assumes and it keeps the address as a permanent
identity (your civilizational-timescale principle) while lineage stays queryable in the manifest.
