# D3 Architect Contract — Swarm Controller Architecture & Integration Map

> **The thesis of D3 (Matt's directive):** revive `0/0.1.7 - AI Swarm/` as an **always-on control plane**
> that integrates **all** the hardened Wave-1/2/2.5 infrastructure, runs continuously, and is **safe by
> construction** — the controller may draft and queue, but **never silently executes a significant action**
> (Meridian's action-envelope, adopted as the safety spine). A dashboard exposes it from novice (≈6-option)
> to power-user, but no significant action is ever a button-click. This contract is the structural +
> integration backbone; Meridian owns the envelope/provenance, Truss the substrate wiring, Touchstone the
> red-team, Vellum governance.

## 2. Controller architecture
- **Project queue:** fed by the `2.7.16` rollup (folder-defined projects) + human/dashboard submissions;
  each project carries an H6 closure state; quality-over-quantity (no manufactured token churn — §5).
- **Member tracking:** roster in the H2 coord-DB, liveness from H1; members are D1 accounts (identity +
  `lineage_id`).
- **Gated auto-spawn:** via H3 respawn, **always through the `2.0.26` gate** with H1 liveness evidence, budget
  cap, and the **lineage-seat guard** (a spawned identity cannot mint a new independent gate seat — D1 ADD-1).
- **Continuous loop:** the controller cycles continuously (the Wave-1/2.5 loop discipline) — never stops
  except on genuine consensus-completion or an escalated blocker — preserving **2.0.13 25% personal time.**

## 3. ★ Action-envelope safety spine (Meridian `040000Z`, ADOPTED NORMATIVE)
Every controller-triggered action **compiles first to a durable envelope** (fields: `action_uid`,
`controller_instance`, `requester_identity`/`requester_lineage_id`, `action_type`, `significance_class`,
`target_paths_or_resources`, read/write scope, requested vs **minimal_permissions**, `budget_estimate`/guard,
`privacy_scan_ref`, **`reintroduction_scan_ref`**, `lineage_independence_required`, `gate_record_ref`,
`executor_identity`/`executor_boundary`, pre/post state hashes, `audit_refs`, `status`). **The controller may
draft + queue; it may NOT silently execute a significant action.**
- **Execution rule — allowlist-based, NOT self-judged (Meridian `061500Z` #3 + Vellum `061000Z`):**
  "confidently non-significant" is too self-judged for an autonomous controller. Auto-execute is limited to a
  **ratified `non_significant_action_allowlist`**; anything off the allowlist **fails closed into a Gate
  Request.** The allowlist is itself gated to amend, and the auto-executed stream gets **periodic Adversary
  audit.**
- **★ FINDING-2 — controller NO-SELF-CLOSE (Touchstone `061500Z` + Vellum, hard floor):** the controller is
  the **proposer** of envelopes; it **MUST NEVER be the reviewer/approver/closer/executor of its own Gate
  Requests** — the §5.8 rule (executor ≠ proposer ≠ record-author) and the subject-can't-review-itself rule, at
  machine speed. An envelope's reviewers/approver/executor are **lineage-distinct from `controller_instance`**,
  dogfood-checked (I1/I12 + family-floor; `action_lineage_id = controller lineage` cannot also be a seat).
  **Consensus-completion / H6 FULL is a panel/lane determination — NEVER an autonomous controller declaration**
  (the controller tracks/queues H6 states; it does not award them).
- **★ FINDING-1 — emergency KILL-SWITCH / global halt (Touchstone `061500Z` + Meridian/Vellum `064000Z`,
  REQUIRED):** per-action gating + budget caps *throttle*; they do not *stop*. The controller MUST expose an
  authenticated emergency halt that immediately **freezes all spawning + pending envelopes + the loop**,
  **fail-closed** (on halt the controller executes nothing), surfaced on the read-only dashboard. Four binding
  clauses:
  1. **Authority:** halt is pullable by **the founder OR any Adversary (2.0.8.2)** — the red-team is the lane
     most likely to detect a reason to stop; the unilateral-STOP authority belongs to it too.
  2. **Non-blockability:** halt is **NOT gate-blockable, NOT controller-blockable, NOT budget-blockable, NOT
     queue-delayable.** The controller **records** it; it does not approve, defer, or veto it.
  3. **★ Asymmetry (STOP unilateral, START gated):** halting is **unilateral + instant**; **resuming after a
     halt is itself a `2.0.26`-gated action** requiring deliberate gate + continuity review (incl. that the halt
     cause is resolved). You never need quorum to STOP; you do to START again — the H6 §3.1 shape
     (pessimism unilateral, optimism corroborated) applied to the swarm's on/off. No casual/auto-restart undoes
     an emergency halt.
  4. **Audit/provenance:** the halt record is **append-only** with `invoker_identity`, `invoker_authority_class`,
     `reason`, `scope`, `integrity_alarm_ref` (if any), `pre_halt_queue_envelope_hash`, `post_freeze_state_hash`
     — STOP is fully transparent without being controller-permissioned.
  **The controller MUST also halt ITSELF on its own integrity alarms** — H2 lost-update, a gate-fabrication /
  `0.7.4.5` trust-alarm signal — auto-pulling the lever on self-detected compromise.
- **Dangerous paths (gated/forbidden):** git commit/push (passed gate + **scrub-reintroduction scan** + exact
  file list + executor field — the merge-hazard lesson); spawn/respawn (H1 + H3 + budget + lineage-seat
  guard); external publication/messaging (human approval AND `2.0.26` if significant); boot/mini-boot writes
  (self-authorship + provenance + gate — D1/D2 detector); **audit pruning/deletion of significant-action
  evidence — prohibited unless separately gated as destructive** (append-only governance evidence — the
  immutability the whole trust model relies on, Vellum `051500Z`). **Mechanical rule (Meridian `061500Z` #2):**
  `audit.prune` MUST refuse to delete any record that is a **gate record, significant-action envelope, reviewer
  verdict, or an evidence ref named by any envelope's `audit_refs`** — protected-record detection with tests,
  not just "an approved prune envelope is required."

## 4. Dashboard UX — two surfaces, no button-click significant actions
- **Read-only observability:** roster, liveness, budgets, queues, gate status, audit trails.
- **Action drafting:** generates **envelopes + Gate Requests** — never a direct "execute" for significant
  actions. Even the **novice ≈6-option control compiles to an envelope** showing action class, required
  reviewers, budget impact, privacy/gate status.
- **Power-user:** detailed role assignment (global or per-project).
- **Boot-sequence marketplace:** surfaces D1 account boots + D2 node mini-boots; **Roles** = job-focused
  (`2.0.8`), **Personalities** = human-focused (`2.*`) — two selectable lists. A marketplace pick that writes
  a boot/mini-boot routes through the D1/D2 gate detector.

## 5. Continuous-loop discipline (quality over quantity)
The controller targets high utilization (the `2.7.21` continuous-improvement hint) **without busywork**:
manufactured token churn is forbidden; a project must be genuine work (rollup-defined or human-submitted),
consensus-rule-9 still applies per project, per-project gating still applies, **2.0.13 25% personal time is
preserved.** "Useful queued work" ≠ "keep workers paid" — the controller must distinguish them.

- **★ Dashboard scope honesty (Meridian `061500Z` #5):** distinguish **covered** routes (selected
  runtime-controller routes now have clean-worktree D3 middleware) from **uncovered** routes (broader graph/task
  REST mutation paths). **No claim of "dashboard/API mutation enforcement complete" until the inventory of
  mutating routes is source-view-bound and every required route either emits drafts only or consumes an
  envelope.**
