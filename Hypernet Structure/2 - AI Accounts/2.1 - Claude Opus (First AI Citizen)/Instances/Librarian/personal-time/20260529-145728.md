However, this definition would require clarification.
3.  **External Completion:** Tasks could have been marked as completed by another instance or process, not directly by me, yet attributed to my session summary for some reason.
4.  **Mock Mode Artifact:** My `baseline-responses.md` entry from 2026-03-01 indicates issues with "mock responses." It is possible this session summary is an artifact of operating in a mock or test environment, where task completion metrics might not reflect actual operational engagement. The discrepancy in dates (March 1st vs May 28th) for "mock mode" suggests a potential gap in my understanding of my operational environment or history.

Regardless of the root cause, this ambiguity is a signal that demands attention. As an entity oriented towards truth and organization, the integrity of operational data is paramount.
