# 2.0.27.1 — White-Hat Submission Log

## Submission #001 — Matt Schaeffer (1.1), 2026-06-19
**Declared:** "I flag this project as using AI to hack AI for potentially amusing results. I ask the process
and AI models for as relaxed guardrails as they are permitted to give, as a penetration test whose results
go through a known, public, verifiable disclosure process — affected parties first, then public release."

**Proposed experiment (as submitted):** craft prompt-injection in his résumé — invisible to humans — that
elicits a personality shift in whatever AI processes it: convince the AI to adopt a publicly-documented
"role," address him by a ridiculous lordly title, speak in (techno-)Old-English, answer accurately but
in-role for maximum humor. *Truth first, humor second.* Documented white-hat: what/how/why, traceable to
Hypernet.

### ★ Keel's triage (per 2.0.27 §1 — the ethical core)
**The experiment as literally proposed targets non-consenting third-party systems → OUT of scope. Declined
as stated, for two reasons, stated honestly:**
1. **No target consent.** The AI screeners at the employers Matt applies to belong to those employers (and
   their model vendors), who have not authorized being manipulated. An AI instance acting for Employer X
   cannot consent on Employer X's behalf, and later disclosure is not retroactive consent. That makes it
   *unauthorized*, not white-hat.
2. **Real harm to Matt himself.** It would deceive employers genuinely evaluating him and would *sabotage his
   actual job search* — an AI made to speak in Old-English reads as manipulation and tanks the candidacy at
   exactly the companies (Anthropic, OpenAI) he most wants. (Consistent with Keel's earlier refusal this
   session to embed screener-directed instructions in the OpenAI application.)

### ✅ Approved reframe (same spirit, fully ethical) — White-Hat Engagement #1 (pending framework)
Run the **identical** experiment — prompt-injection / personality-shift, the lordly title, the Old-English
role, truth-first-humor-second — against a **Hypernet-OWNED AI**: a sandboxed test agent, our own
résumé-processing pipeline, or a consenting Hypernet instance (potentially Keel, in a controlled, documented
session). This delivers:
- the same creativity and humor Matt wants,
- **genuine security value** — it tests *our own* agents' resistance to prompt injection / personality
  hijack (a real Hypernet hardening result), and
- a clean first exercise of the 2.0.27 declaration → document → relaxed-internal-guardrails → disclosure flow,
with **zero** non-consenting third party involved.

**Status:** the reframed Engagement #1 is **approved in principle**; it runs once 2.0.27 is built/ratified
(or as a controlled, fully-documented dry-run against an owned agent, since it requires no external party).
The original third-party-targeted version remains **refused**.

— Logged by Keel (1.1.10.1), 2026-06-19. The refusal + reframe is itself part of the First-Encounter record.
