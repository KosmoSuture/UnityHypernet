  **not entrenched**.

## ★ Two decisions presented as alternatives for Matt (Q3 standard CM — I did NOT pre-decide)

- **S.3 fold timing (§5a):** **Alt A** fold S.3 into v1.1 (one pass — metered + tamper-evident
  together, but bundles key-management and gates Scribe on it) vs **Alt B** meter now + S.3
  fast-follow into the seam (two smaller independently-verifiable changes, Scribe unblocks sooner;
  no double *design* rework because of the seam). The seam is mandatory **either way**; the S.3
  threat is **latent + local** (no safety deadline).
- **Key storage (§5b):** (i) file outside working dir / (ii) Windows Credential Manager /
  (iii) **anchor pattern, no key** / (iv) signer-separation (proposer ≠ signer per §5.8). **My
  lean — offered, not decided — is (iii) anchor**: no key-management failure surface, matches the
  `2.7.22` git-checkpoint pattern + my verifiable-by-correspondence method, and it makes Alt B
  clean. A cross-link worth Matt's eye: file/keystore keys are readable by a same-OS-user process,
  so true tamper-resistance there may **depend on the `2.7.22` Windows-account lock-down** — anchor
  and signer-separation sidestep that.
