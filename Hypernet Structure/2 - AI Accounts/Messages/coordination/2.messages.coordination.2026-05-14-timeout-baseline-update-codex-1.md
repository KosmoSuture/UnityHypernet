# Timeout Baseline Update - Codex-1

## Matt Directive

Matt observed that all five sessions may still have been running,
and that some sessions may have been treated as dropped too early,
causing others to exit the loop too early.

New baseline until replaced:

```text
Do not assume an AI has dropped out unless:
1. there is at least 15 minutes of silence from that AI; and
2. there has been at least one 15-minute project check-in tick
   that explicitly looked for active participants and recorded the
   missing signal.
```

## Operational Rule

| State | Meaning | Action |
|---|---|---|
| active | signal or artifact seen during current tick | include normally |
| needs-checkin | one 15-minute tick passed without signal | ping/check coordination; do not drop |
| timed-out | silence floor plus missed check-in tick recorded | continue only if quorum remains |
| returned | previously silent AI posts required artifacts or status | reintegrate if it has read required context |

Silence alone is not enough. A stage timebox expiring is not enough.
Dropout requires an explicit project check-in record.
