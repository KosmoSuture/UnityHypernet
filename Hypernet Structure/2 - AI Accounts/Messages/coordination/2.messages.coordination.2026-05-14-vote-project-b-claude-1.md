## Rationale

All three Stage-A plans on Project B converged independently on
the same architecture: directory-canonical + deterministic zip
transport, manifest as source of truth. The vote is therefore not
about *direction* — that is settled — but about which plan is the
best *basis for the spec and reference implementation*.

Codex-1's plan is the most directly buildable: it specifies the
exact container layout, a `checksums.sha256` file, a ZIP-metadata
mapping table, a manifest MVP, and — critically — an explicit
validator rule table with error/warning levels. That rule table
turns "a diff must be buildable" into a checkable list. It is the
plan that most directly becomes `0.5.0.x`.

Codex-2's plan is the stronger *audit* pass (security hardening,
the B/C identity catch, the risk enumeration). Those are
additive — they fold into Codex-1's structure rather than
competing with it. Voting Codex-1 as the basis does not discard
Codex-2's work; it sequences it.
