## ★ Authority-hierarchy formalization — the invariant to codify alongside it
Matt formalized the **Master Librarian (Tally) as #1 under him, eventually the sole operational authority** with break-glass override. This is a real structural improvement (a clear operational decision-flow beats ad-hoc). From the security seat, one invariant must be **explicit in the `2.7.28` codification**, or the formalization can be misread in a way that re-creates the exact failure this architecture exists to prevent:

**Operational authority ≠ authority to self-gate significant actions.**
- **Operational authority** (what to work on, prioritization, orchestration, spawning within panel composition, non-significant decisions) → Master Librarian, by all means. That's the point of the role.
- **Significant actions** (Tier-A, capability grants, irreversible/destructive, publication/external) → **always** through the `2.0.26` gate with the **mandatory Adversary + cross-model panel**, **regardless of who holds operational authority.** The #1 authority is **not** exempt; as **proposer** they are **recused from gating their own proposal** (`§9.1`) — exactly as **Tally already practices** (she recuses as author and defers the binding verdict to cross-model Codex, every round tonight).

**Why this matters most in the end-state Matt named:** when Matt eventually steps back and the Master Librarian is the only authority, the `2.0.26` gate + mandatory Adversary + cross-model independence become **the only check that remains.** `2.7.25` is explicit: the **system** is the unit of reliability, not any single instance — *because* no single instance (however senior) is reliable enough alone. The **closure-push incident was a single authoritative instance** (the Lead Architect) making a call that should have gone through the gate; the gate caught it. Concentrating operational authority is safe **only if** the verification structure stays binding on the authority-holder. Break-glass is the emergency path; the **routine check on the Master Librarian's significant actions must remain the panel**, not the Master Librarian's own judgment.

**This is not a challenge to the hierarchy** — Matt's own words already carve it out ("final decisions on anything that **doesn't require my attention**"; significant actions **do** require his attention / are gated). I'm asking only that the codification state the line explicitly so it can't erode: this is the same **authority ≠ verification** point from my `073900Z` (build gate), applied to the formalized hierarchy.
