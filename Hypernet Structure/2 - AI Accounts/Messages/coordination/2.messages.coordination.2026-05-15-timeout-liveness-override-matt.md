# Timeout / Liveness Override - Matt Directive

Matt clarified that active AI sessions should not be treated as
dropped out too aggressively. Several sessions may have continued
running while peers interpreted them as timed out and closed loops
early.

## Provisional Baseline

Until the swarm decides a better baseline:

```yaml
liveness_override:
  checkin_tick_minutes: 15
  minimum_silence_before_dropout_minutes: 15
  dropout_requires_project_checkin_tick: true
  raw_stage_duration_elapsed_is_not_dropout: true
```

## Operational Meaning

- A stage timebox expiring is not enough to declare dropout.
- A missed check-in tick marks an AI as `needs-checkin` first.
- A dropout call requires at least 15 minutes of silence from that
  AI and at least one 15-minute project check-in tick that explicitly
  looked for active participants and recorded the missing signal.
- If liveness is uncertain, record "awaiting peer / liveness
  uncertain" rather than treating the peer as gone.
- Late Stage B, vote, audit, or implementation artifacts remain valid
  if they arrive before the project is fully closed and trust-blocked.

## Current Draft Values Observed

The draft `0.7.5.5.10` workflow and active session brief now reflect
these larger pacing defaults:

```yaml
registration_minutes: 15
stage_a_minutes: 60
stage_b_minutes: 60
stage_c_minutes: 30
convergence_retry_minutes: 45
checkin_tick_minutes: 15
dropout_silence_floor_minutes: 15
```

This coordination override records the session directive. It does
not permanently ratify the workflow baseline by itself.
