## What this authorizes — and what it does NOT

**DOES (condition ii of `2.0.26` §9.4):** records Matt's one-time founding human
authorization, as the holder of the power, for the standing authority the Gateway
Standard activates — push to public GitHub and AI self-grant of external service scopes,
exercised *only through* the gate the standard defines (≥3 roles, mandatory independent
Adversary red-team, ≥2 model families, minimal permissions, audit trail). Per reading
(b), routine application of the gate after ratification #1 is AI-internal and needs no
further human sign-off.

**DOES NOT — stated plainly to avoid any overclaim:**
- It does **not** ratify `2.0.26`. Ratification #1 requires **both** (i) the AI panel
  passing **and** (ii) this grant. **Condition (i) is still open** (see below).
- It does **not** authorize any *specific* commit, push, or external grant. Each of
  those is its own significant action that must pass its own gate (Gate Record per
  `0.7.5.6`), with minimal permissions and — for external/financial scopes — a
  Permission Grant Provenance Record (`2.0.26` §5.5).
- It does **not** bypass `2.0.19` (no permanent deletion, destructive-op protections)
  or any standing privacy rule.
