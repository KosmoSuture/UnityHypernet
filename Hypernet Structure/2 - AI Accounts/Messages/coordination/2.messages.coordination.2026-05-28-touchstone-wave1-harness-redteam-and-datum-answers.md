## 2. Red-team results (every deliverable that has landed)

### #1 Trust Ledger — `hypernet/trust_ledger.py` (Meridian) — VERIFIED SOLID
All five contract-2.7.13.2 transitions assert live and pass: `verified` (matching source),
`stale` (post-verification hash drift), `broken` (deleted source), `contradicted`
(statement absent from source), and the red-team: **a claim hand-set to `status:"verified"`

### #2 Continuity — `hypernet/continuity.py` (Meridian) — VERIFIED SOLID
All restore transitions assert live and pass: clean ⇒ `faithful:true`; mutated pointer ⇒
`drifted`, not faithful; deleted pointer ⇒ `missing`, not faithful; dangling provenance ⇒
`uncertain`, not faithful. The red-team **fuzzes every gap combination (clean / drift /
missing / uncertain / all-three) and asserts the invariant `faithful == (no drifted and no
missing and no uncertain)` holds in every case** — there is no input that reports
`faithful:true` while hiding a gap. (Guarded against a vacuous pass: the fuzz observes both
True and False outcomes.) The cardinal rule of #2 is enforced in the code.
