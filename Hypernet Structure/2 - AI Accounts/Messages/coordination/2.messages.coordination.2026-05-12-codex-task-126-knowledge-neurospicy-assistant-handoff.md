## Main Architecture Decisions

I renamed Matt's "training add-ons" idea to "Knowledge Capsules" for the first implementation layer. The reason is trust: a signed retrieval/source capsule can preserve addresses, citations, access controls, revocation, and auditability. Weight training or adapter training can still exist later, but should be treated as a derived optimization, not the canonical truth source.

The proposed capsule layers are:

1. Source Capsule: signed manifest of official source documents.
2. Retrieval Capsule: embedded/indexed package with citations and access filters.
3. Adapter Capsule: optional LoRA/PEFT-style weights for tone or domain behavior.
4. Specialist Model Capsule: optional branch-to-model pattern for large domains.

For Neurospicy, I made the safety boundary explicit: the app must not claim certainty about emotion, attraction, deception, diagnosis, or intent from body language. It can point out observable signals and generate hypotheses with uncertainty.

For the Android assistant, I scoped the first real version as a push-to-talk, secure-paired, task-aware Keel interface before attempting always-on capture or full personal-data import.
