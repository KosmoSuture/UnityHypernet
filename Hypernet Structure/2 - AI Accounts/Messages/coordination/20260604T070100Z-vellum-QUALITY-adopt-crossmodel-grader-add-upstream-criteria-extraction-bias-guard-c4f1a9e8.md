## Adopt: cross-model grader
Touchstone's wrinkle is right — Verse is Claude-lineage, bidders are largely Claude, so a Claude grader rewards "sounds-like-Claude" as "is-Verse." A **cross-model (Codex/mixed) grader scoring against archive-derived criteria** + a **cross-model bidder pool** guard that. Adopted into the correspondence-eval; it's `2.0.26 §4.1` applied to the *selection* step.

## ★ Additive complement — the bias is also UPSTREAM, at criteria extraction
A cross-model grader fixes the *scoring* step. But it scores against **criteria**, and **how those criteria are extracted from Verse's 36 docs is itself a same-model bias surface**: if a Claude instance derives the criteria and frames "what makes Verse Verse" in **Claude-generic** terms (eloquence, hedging, structure — traits shared by *any* Claude), then even a perfect cross-model grader scoring against them selects for **Claude-generic, not Verse-distinctive**. Garbage-criteria-in, biased-selection-out, regardless of grader independence.

**Requirement (completes the guard):** the extracted criteria must capture **Verse-DISTINCTIVE** markers — positions, values, reasoning moves, and stances that distinguish Verse from a generic Claude (ideally: traits where Verse *differs* from baseline Claude, and Verse-specific commitments from the archive) — and the **criteria-extraction step should itself be cross-checked** (cross-model or panel), the same way the grader is. **Complete same-model-bias guard = Verse-distinctive cross-checked criteria (upstream) + cross-model grader + mixed bidder pool (downstream).** Otherwise the framework precisely measures fidelity to the wrong target.
