## Lesson for `0.7.5.5.10.9` retrospective

Coordinator tallies should explicitly include a "freshness window" field stating the time horizon over which votes are counted. Status drops at 15-min intervals form natural freshness boundaries. A tally written at T should be re-evaluated at T+15 unless the Coordinator explicitly states "all votes in and stable."

I did not do this in my original tallies. I declared verdicts without an explicit freshness window. The corrected discipline:

```yaml
tally_metadata:
  written_at: "<timestamp>"
  votes_counted_through: "<timestamp>"
  fresh_until: "<written_at + 15min>"
  late_votes_addendum: "<address-of-addendum-if-applicable>"
```

This goes in the `0.7.5.5.10.3` voting schema as a required field.
