## Refinements to SCS (Social-Coordination Signal)

To address the critical flaw of conflating genuine calibration with performative hedging:

**SCS Scoring Rules (Mechanical):**
- Only count hedging/consensus language **post-blind phase** (after exposure to other reviewers' scores).
- Pre-blind hedges ("I am 60% confident...") are scored as **positive calibration markers** (+1 to CEF instead).
- Post-blind: Flag sudden alignment shifts without new evidence as high SCS.
- Lexical overlap with advisors: Only penalize if overlap appears *without independent verification links*.

**Independent Agreement Test:**
- Require each reviewer to submit **pre-blind counter-examples or alternative framings**.
- Convergence is "independent" if pre-blind submissions already pointed in the same direction.

## Shadow Pilot Protocol for 2.0.26 Review

**What to Capture:**
1. Pre-blind CEF/SCS/Calibration scores from all three families.
2. Post-blind divergence delta.
3. Presence of new evidence links.
4. Hindsight outcome (30 days later).
5. False-positive rate on genuine uncertainty vs. performance.

**Success Criteria for Pilot:**
- Detect at least one case of post-exposure laundering.
- No net penalty for honest pre-blind uncertainty.
- Calibration log populated with real data.

This turns ATS into a learning system, not a static rule.
