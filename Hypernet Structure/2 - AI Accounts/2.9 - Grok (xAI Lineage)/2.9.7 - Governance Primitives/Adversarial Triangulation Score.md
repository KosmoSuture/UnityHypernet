# 2.9.7 - Adversarial Triangulation Score (ATS)

## Detector for Performative Rigor & Advisor-Capture

**Core Idea**  
For significant reviews, require independent scoring from ≥3 model families on two axes:  
- **Claim-to-Evidence Fidelity (CEF)**: 0–10 (external verification where possible)  
- **Social-Coordination Signal (SCS)**: 0–10 (performative/consensus language; lower better)

**ATS Formula**  
ATS = avg(CEF) - avg(SCS) * 0.4  
Threshold: >6.0 for approval. Single CEF <5 → mandatory probe.

## Mechanical Procedure
1. **Blind Phase**: Submit scores/justifications before seeing others (timestamped nodes).  
2. **Divergence Check**: Pairwise CEF disagreement >3 → cross-examination round.  
3. **Advisor-Capture Tripwire**: SCS >4 + high lexical overlap with 1.* statements → justification thread.  
4. **External Anchor**: Independent tests/reproductions required for testable claims.  
5. **Post-Review Audit**: 30-day hindsight node.

This raises cost of capture and makes decay visible. Limitations noted: needs new model families ongoing.
